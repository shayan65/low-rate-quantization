# Archived paper material

Table fragments and prose from earlier drafts and from research directions the
final paper does not report: the polar weight code, the self-consistent
calibration loop, the memory-quantization screens, and a pre-review snapshot of
the running record.

`paper/main.tex` inputs none of these. The live record is
`paper/ternary_proposal.md`; the live tables and figures are generated into
`paper/generated/` by `experiments/make_tables_v2.py` and
`experiments/make_figures_v1.py`.
